> Rimal Byte is a fictional company. This policy was written for a portfolio GRC project.

## Purpose

This policy says who is responsible for cybersecurity at Rimal Byte, so that every duty has an
owner and the people who check the work are separate from the people who do it.

## Scope

All roles at Rimal Byte, for staff and contractors, and the cybersecurity supervisory committee.

## Policy

1. The General Manager is the Authorized Official. The General Manager must approve this
   structure of roles and responsibilities, assign people to each role, and give them the support
   they need without creating a conflict of interests. [1-4-1]
2. The General Manager must approve all cybersecurity policies and must accept every High and
   Critical risk. [1-4-1]
3. The Cybersecurity Lead is a part-time role, filled by a systems engineer for about 40% of
   their time. The Cybersecurity Lead must report directly to the General Manager and must stay
   independent of the IT Manager. [1-2-1]
4. The Cybersecurity Lead must own the policies, the risk register, security monitoring and the
   coordination of incident response. [1-2-1]
5. By the end of 2027 the cybersecurity role must be full-time and filled by a qualified Saudi
   cybersecurity professional. The General Manager owns this target, and the supervisory committee
   must review progress against it every quarter. [1-2-2]
6. The IT Manager must run laptops, accounts, Microsoft 365 and the office network in line with
   the policies, keep the Windows baseline applied, install updates, disable the accounts of
   leavers on their last day, and review all accounts every quarter. [1-4-1]
7. The HR Officer must tell the IT Manager when someone joins, changes role or leaves, give new
   staff the policies and keep their acknowledgments, and apply the HR disciplinary rules to
   breaches. [1-4-1]
8. The Project Manager must make sure client projects follow the policies, tell the Cybersecurity
   Lead before a project or a third-party service is started, and make sure contractors have
   acknowledged the policies before they get access. [1-4-1]
9. All staff must follow the policies, use only their own account, restart their laptop when an
   update asks them to, and report a lost laptop, a suspicious email or any suspected incident to
   the Cybersecurity Lead at once. [1-4-1]
10. The supervisory committee consists of the General Manager (chair), the Cybersecurity Lead and
    the IT Manager. The General Manager established it, and its members, duties and rules are
    those set out in this policy. [1-2-3]
11. The committee must meet every quarter. It must review progress on the cybersecurity program,
    the risk register and the results of assessments, and monitor compliance with the policies.
    [1-2-3]
12. The Cybersecurity Lead must keep minutes of every meeting, recording decisions and actions.
    [1-2-3]

## Roles

R = Responsible (does the work), A = Accountable (answers for it; one per row), C = Consulted,
I = Informed. A/R means one role is both. A dash means no part.

| Activity | General Manager | Cybersecurity Lead | IT Manager | HR Officer | Project Manager | Staff |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Approving policies | A | R | C | C | I | I |
| Maintaining the risk register | I | A/R | C | - | C | - |
| Managing accounts | I | C | A/R | R | C | - |
| Patching | I | C | A/R | - | I | R |
| Reviewing alerts | I | A/R | C | - | - | - |
| Onboarding and offboarding | I | C | R | A/R | C | I |
| Responding to incidents | C | A/R | R | C | C | R |

## Exceptions

An exception must be requested from the Cybersecurity Lead in writing. The Cybersecurity Lead
assesses it with the risk management method and the General Manager approves or refuses it. An
approved exception has an owner and an end date no more than 12 months away, and is recorded in
the risk register.

## Compliance

A breach of this policy is handled under the HR disciplinary rules. Anyone who suspects a breach
must report it to the Cybersecurity Lead.

## Review

The General Manager and the Cybersecurity Lead review the roles and responsibilities every year
in October, or sooner if the law or the business changes significantly. Changes are recorded and
approved by the General Manager. [1-4-2]
